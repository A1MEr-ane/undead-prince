STYLE_EPIC (for ending key art; replaces the default STYLE block for endings only):
Luminous high-detail digital painting in the manner of epic fantasy webcomic cover art: refined, beautiful, melancholic. Realistic adult proportions; elegant, delicate faces with calm or sorrowful restraint; long flowing hair, capes and fabric moved by wind; armour with fine engraved detail and soft metallic sheen. Painterly soft-edged rendering with selective crisp edges on faces and silhouettes; NO heavy cartoon ink outlines, no flat cel shading, no cute or childlike faces.
LIGHT IS THE SUBJECT: one dominant motivated light source (sun shaft through a high window, sunset, dawn, a lantern) glowing through haze; bloom and volumetric god rays; drifting light particles (embers, dust, sea spray, ash, petals) catching the light; deep atmospheric shadows; highlights reserved for faces, crown, blade and the one symbolic object. Cyan-green soulfire glows only on the undead; ivory-gold or natural sunset light on the living and the city.
POWER IN THE COMPOSITION: camera angle and placement state who holds power: low angles and elevated thrones for the ruler, figures dwarfed by vast architecture or sea, ranks of soldiers as repeating silhouettes fading into haze, the key figure isolated by light. Strong diagonals and depth: dark foreground silhouettes, midground figures, background receding into luminous atmosphere.
PALETTE: muted, desaturated cool shadows (ink-navy, sea-grey, slate) against warm or cyan light; one or two saturated accents (purple cape, crimson sash, gold crown).
NEGATIVE: ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT of any kind in the image: no titles, captions, letters, numbers, signatures, labels, banners with writing, inscriptions or UI. No chibi, no cute, no bright flat cartoon colours, no flat even lighting, no heavy black comic outlines, no centered snapshot composition, no text, no logos, no names of existing games, studios or artists.
