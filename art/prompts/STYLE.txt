STYLE (prepend to every prompt, do not edit):
Original hand-painted narrative game illustration. Digital opaque gouache with HEAVY BLACK INK CONTOURS: the outer silhouette line is the thickest (about 4-6 px at 1024 px width), interior structure lines thinner, line pressure visibly varies, brush edges slightly irregular. Elongated, angular, stylised adult anatomy; sharp graphic shapes; expressive face; confident, readable pose with a clear line of action. Background is a dark ink-navy / sea-grey painted field with loose strokes, so the figure is the brightest, most saturated thing in the image. Limited palette per figure: two or three main colours plus the rim-light colour. Clean value grouping, uncluttered silhouettes, no busy scratch texture.
NEGATIVE: no 3D render, no low-poly or faceted triangles, no photorealism, no plastic or glossy surfaces, no soft airbrush, no anime moe face, no chibi proportions (unless the prompt explicitly asks for tiny battle troops), no text except where the prompt asks for it, no logos, no watermarks, no names of existing games, studios or artists.
