STYLE (prepend to every character prompt, do not edit) — "Refined ink", approved 2026-11-25:
Premium hand-painted character art for a narrative game. Mature, realistic adult proportions and believable faces with subtle, restrained acting. Confident dark contour lines that vary from hairline-thin to bold, used sparingly where form turns and at the outer silhouette, never a uniform cartoon outline. Painterly gouache rendering with deliberate edge control: crisp edges on the face, hands, crown and key props; lost edges where the figure melts into shadow. One motivated key light from a believable direction, soft and natural, with deep shadow covering a large part of the figure; a thin, precise rim light in the accent colour. Restrained palette: ink-navy and charcoal darks, the character's two or three costume colours, one accent light, small gold details. Fine material detail on metal, cloth, bone and skin, but calm and uncluttered overall. Dark, quiet background with generous negative space, composed like an editorial poster rather than a centred snapshot.
NEGATIVE: absolutely no text, letters, labels or watermarks unless the prompt explicitly asks for view labels; no chibi, no cute or childlike faces, no thick uniform cartoon outlines, no flat cel shading, no low-poly or 3D render look, no plastic shine, no harsh spotlight beams, no names of existing games, studios or artists.
