# B.4.2R — Core character revision / 核心人物返修

**WORKING — user review pending. / 暂定，待评审。**

## Visual standard / 视觉规范

Every character requires a hero concept and a separate, legible front/side/back turnaround in a neutral pose, matched scale, consistent anatomy and costume. The final modelling sheet uses orthographic projection, one ground line, no perspective, no hidden props over the body, and no cropped limbs. Separate equipment, expression and material studies when required. All labels inside art are English. Narrative images are not modelling sheets. Generated concepts require manual consistency checks; a label is not proof of orthographic accuracy.

每名角色须有主概念图和独立清晰的正、侧、背三视图；中性姿势、相同比例、形体及服饰一致。最终建模参考使用正交投影、统一地面线、无透视、道具不遮挡主体、不裁切肢体。必要时另做道具、表情与材质图。图内仅英文标注。故事插画不能代替建模图；生成图须人工校验，不能仅凭标注认定正交准确。

## The Ferryman / 渡魂人

![The Ferryman](art/ART-B4-FERRYMAN-R1.png)

His authority should come from stillness, ceremonial dress and the absence of a human body, not rags or dirt. The hood contains darkness rather than a confirmed skull or human face. The hem dissolves into translucent sea-mist in a restrained teal light, and no feet are visible. Maintain the oar and the single lantern. The layered cloth may remain opaque in its upper portion; lower edges can be a separate material or effect layer. The sheet is a new visual proposal, not a declaration of his supernatural identity. Confirm the rear fastening, sleeve geometry and lantern grip before production.

他的威仪来自静止、完整的祭服和非实体感，而非破布与污垢。兜帽内是不可辨认的阴影，不确认人脸或骷髅。袍尾在克制青绿光中雾化半透明，不露脚。保留船桨与单盏灯。上部织物可保持不透明，下缘使用独立材质或特效层。本图仅为新视觉提案，不揭示其真实身份。生产前核对背部扣饰、袖部结构与持灯方式。
