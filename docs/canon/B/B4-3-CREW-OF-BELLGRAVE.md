## CHAR_CAPTAIN — Captain Bernard / 伯纳德卫队长

![Captain Bernard concept and turnaround](art/ART-B4-BERNARD-R1.png)

**Design.** A broad, grounded former naval officer, his familiar drill and meticulously kept armour outlasting his body. A closed naval helm with a narrow muted teal slit; aged ivory and bronze plates, a dark purple mantle, a crown badge and worn shield. No horns or visible human face. The sword and shield are independent props, not mandatory attachments to the base mesh. His stance should express discipline rather than menace.

**设计。** 宽厚沉稳的前海军军官，熟悉的军纪与精心维护的甲胄比肉身更持久。封闭海军盔、狭窄的低亮青绿视缝、旧象牙白与暗铜甲片、深紫披风、王冠徽与旧盾。无骨角、不露活人脸。剑盾作为独立道具，不强制并入主体模型。站姿强调纪律与稳重。

**Review.** The sheet contains three full-body views, separate weapons and helmet studies. Check side-to-front shoulder thickness, rear cloak fastening, the exact fractured-crown emblem against the prince reference, and foot alignment. Hero crop is not a full-body modelling view. Do not treat the generated turnaround as a measured orthographic drawing. 

**验收。** 图中具备三幅全身视图、独立武器与头盔参考。正式建模前核对肩甲厚度、背部披风连接、碎王冠标记与王子原图的一致性、足底齐平。主展示为局部特写，不替代全身图；不得将生成三视图视为已测量正交图。

## CHAR_STITCHER — Matilda / 玛蒂尔达（待生成）

**Standalone brief.** A compact, sturdy undead field surgeon, age 38 at death. Old naval clothing under a practical brown leather apron, rolled sleeves, gloves, a belt with an instrument roll. A few clean stitches on ashen skin and a restrained turquoise seam or exposed bone at one wrist establish her undead state without gore. No mechanical prosthesis unless separately authorised; she is a surgeon, not a generic blacksmith. Hair tied out of her work; relaxed, capable expression. Hero pose with curved needle and thread; front, side and back in the same neutral stance, props removed. Separate instruments: needle, thread spool, shears, tool roll. Brown, faded navy, ivory, bone grey and restrained teal.

**独立出图规格。** 38岁身亡的紧凑结实亡灵军医。旧海军服外套实用棕皮围裙，卷袖、手套、腰间器械卷。灰白皮肤少量整齐缝线，腕部露出少量骨质或青绿冥火缝隙，避免血腥。未经另行确定不增加机械义肢；她是军医而非普通铁匠。扎起头发方便工作，表情从容专业。主立绘手持弯针及线，三视图同一中性站姿、卸下道具。弯针、线轴、剪刀及器械卷独立图。棕色、褪色海军蓝、米白、骨灰与少量青绿。

S04 — A Hand for Knots: a repair should be a tool for the patient's wishes, not just a way of returning soldiers to combat. / 关联 S04 打绳结的手：修复应当满足伤员自己的愿望，不仅用于重返战场。

## CHAR_MAGE — Oswin / 奥斯温（待生成）

**Standalone brief.** A thin lich scholar, age 43 at death. Clearly readable bone face and small teal eye lights. Faded short scholar's hood and narrow layered robes; visibly solid feet and a hunched, asymmetrical scholarly silhouette distinguish him from the floating, faceless Ferryman. A slate, annotated manuscript and two or three detached floating fragments; no ornate halo, crown or giant combat staff. Neutral front/side/back views remove floating pages so garment geometry stays readable. Separate slate and manuscript. Faded ink blue, grey cloth, old parchment and bone.

**独立出图规格。** 43岁身亡的瘦长巫妖学者。可辨认骨质面孔及微弱青绿眼火；褪色短学者兜帽、窄层叠长袍，有实体足部、略佝偻且不对称的学者剪影，与悬浮无脸的渡魂人区分。石板、批注手稿及少量独立漂浮残页；无光环、王冠及夸张战斗法杖。三视图移除漂浮纸页，露出服装结构；石板手稿另列。褪墨蓝、灰布、旧纸、骨白。

S05 — The Error in the Margin: show an investigator who is curious even when ashamed, not a sinister court magician. / 关联 S05 页边的错误：表现即使惭愧仍具求知欲的研究者，不做邪恶术士。

## CHAR_BUGLER — Pip / 皮普（待生成）

**Standalone brief.** A young adult undead sailor, nineteen at death; small lively silhouette, not a child. Short naval tunic, boots, simple sailor scarf, small broken-crown pennant and oversized brass bugle. A stylised skull face or restrained partial skeletal features, teal eye-light, with a readable mischievous expression; no gore. Keep the bugle larger than ordinary equipment but detachable. Hero pose animated, three views neutral with hands empty and consistent head and boot heights. Separate pennant and bugle. Faded navy, rust red, brass, bone and teal.

**独立出图规格。** 19岁身亡的年轻成年亡灵水手，小巧活泼而非儿童。短海军衣、靴、简洁水手领巾、小碎王冠旗、略夸张的铜号。卡通骷髅脸或克制的局部骷髅特征，青绿眼火，调皮的表情可读，不血腥。铜号尺寸略夸张且可拆卸。主立绘动作活泼，三视图中性站姿、空手、头顶与靴底对齐。旗帜和铜号独立。褪色海军蓝、铁锈红、黄铜、骨白与青绿。
