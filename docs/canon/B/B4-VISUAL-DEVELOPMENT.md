# B.4.1 — The Faces of Alderreach / 海国群像：人物视觉设定第一批

**WORKING · Visual development, not approved production assets. / 暂定视觉开发，非正式生产资产。**

## 1. Visual canon / 视觉基准

The supplied undead Edmund is the identity and proportion reference. Use stylised low-poly geometry, readable silhouettes, broad planes, restrained surface wear and a limited palette. Do not replace the existing undead model or interpret this sheet as orthographic modelling reference. Living royal garments are intact at the anchor or in pre-wreck scenes. The sun device belongs to the royal state; Edmund’s personal crown device is distinct. The regency adds the red sash. Soulfire is cyan-green; living characters must not acquire it merely because the world is dark fantasy.

用户提供的亡魂埃德蒙原图为身份与比例基准。采用低多边形、清晰剪影、大块面、克制磨损与有限色彩。不得用概念板替换原亡灵模型，也不能视为正交建模三视图。生前王室服饰须完整；王室国家日纹与王子私人王冠纹须区分，摄政红绶带单独存在。冥火统一青绿，活人不因黑暗奇幻背景而带有冥火。
