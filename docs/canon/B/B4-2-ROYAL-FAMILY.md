# B.4.2 — Before the Crown Broke / 王冠破碎之前

**WORKING · Concept review, not approved production art. / 暂定概念审阅，非正式生产图。**

## Royal parents / 王室父母

![King Aldric and Queen Beatrice](art/ART-B4-PARENTS-01.png)

The royal portraits use the core cast study’s muted ivory, aged gold and broad faceted planes. Aldric carries the state sun device, a sealed document and a sword; his deep-purple lining connects him visually to Edmund. Beatrice’s marine blue-grey mantle and wave ornament recall her coastal upbringing. Her book is a proposed visual prop, not a newly established plot clue. The late king is approximately fifty-five at his death; Beatrice’s exact age is not fixed. Neither figure is undead. Keep the king’s crown and the prince’s personal crown badge distinct.

父母概念板沿用核心三人板的象牙白、旧金和大块面低多边形。阿尔德里克使用国家日纹、印封公文与佩剑，深紫内衬和埃德蒙形成视觉联系。贝阿特丽斯的海灰蓝披肩和波纹装饰呼应沿海出身。书本是暂定视觉道具，并非新增剧情线索。国王去世时约55岁；王后确切年龄尚未确定。两人均为生前形象。王室国家日纹与王子私人王冠纹继续区分。

## Family portrait study / 家庭肖像构图草案

![Royal household composition study](art/ART-B4-FAMILY-01.png)

Proposed staging: about eight years before the anchor, after Edmund’s first patrol and before Beatrice’s death; Edmund eighteen, Eleanor twelve, Aldric approximately forty-seven. A family portrait in a Sunhold palace hall is a permissible visual framing, not a new historical event. The model produced a composite sheet with incompatible captions; only the text-free picture area is retained as a composition study. It is not approved: Eleanor’s red sash and insignia must be replaced with ordinary princess clothing, Edmund’s age and height must read as an eighteen-year-old naval officer, Aldric should appear younger than his final portrait, and the state device should follow the existing eight-point sun. No regency insignia, no wreck damage and no soul-fire. Do not import incidental text or symbols from discarded generations.

暂定分镜时间：锚点前约八年，王子首次巡防之后、王后去世之前；埃德蒙18岁，埃莉诺12岁，阿尔德里克约47岁。日耀王城宫廷里的家庭肖像属于美术表现提案，不新增历史事件。生成结果混入不符合设定的文字，本库只保留裁取的无字画面作为构图参考。尚未通过审阅：妹妹红绶带和服饰标识须更换为普通公主服装，王子应体现18岁青年军官的年龄与身高，国王应比晚年肖像年轻，国家纹章须回归八芒太阳。无摄政标记、沉船损伤或冥火。废弃图的文字和符号不得纳入正史。
