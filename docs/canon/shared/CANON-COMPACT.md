# Compact canon / 精简创作基准

**Read first.** This compact guide is an index to the active material, not a replacement for the detailed Bible. / 先读本页。它是现行资料的索引，不替代详细总纲。

## Locked / 已定
- An assassinated prince returns with an undead army. Offensive island assault, troop classes, roguelike builds and recurring narrative. Fixed opening, branching campaign to the capital. / 遭谋杀的王子率亡灵攻城；兵种与肉鸽构筑结合；固定开场、分支路线进入王都。
- The anchor follows the wreck, the father's death and the sister's coronation. Only the Prince retains personally experienced memories; the Ferryman knows erased outcomes in an undisclosed way. Ordinary people reset. The Ferryman's knowledge is recognised within the first two expeditions, then no repeated surprise. / 锚点在沉船、父亲去世、妹妹加冕后；仅王子保留亲历记忆，渡魂人以不明方式知晓被抹去的结果，普通人全部重置。前两轮确认渡魂人知情，其后不反复惊讶。
- Every ordinary outcome returns automatically. Knowledge and learned techniques persist, present-history assets and consent do not. / 普通结局必然回溯；知识及习得技艺保留，本轮实物与人物同意不保留。
- The sister is the legitimate successor, initially controlled by the Council; she is a person the Prince loves and has her own agency. / 妹妹合法继位，初期受议会控制，是王子的牵挂，有自主意志。
- Three separated choices inside the capital determine eight success outcomes. Failure E00. Unlock stages sequentially. True Ending: terminate the pact, depart, roll credits, mark save completed. New save starts over. / 王都内分三次决定，产生八种成功结局，另有失败E00；依次解锁。真结局解约离去、播放演职员表、封档，新存档从头开始。
- English first; complete natural Chinese counterpart; visual labels in English. Names provisional. / 英文原稿、完整中文对照、图内英文；人名地名可修订。

## Current working decisions / 现行暂定
- Alderreach is a large maritime kingdom centred on the Crownsea. Sunhold is the eastern political and supernatural centre. The original convoy was sailing outward for relief, diverted to Bellgrave, and sank there. The undead fleet starts with salvage craft; it must secure navigable approaches, not conquer every island. / 海洋王国围绕王冠海，东岸王都；赈粮舰队向外岛航行，被诱导至沉钟湾并在此沉没。亡灵船队从打捞船艇起步，夺取通道而非征服每寸国土。
- The Seal originally helped during a catastrophic winter, but now consumes complete souls to sustain local artificial daylight and warmth. Ordinary necromancy uses residual soulfire. The late king authorised a coercive exception but was not part of the murder. / 秘印起于灾冬，如今消耗完整灵魂维持局部人工白昼光热；普通招魂用残余冥火。先王批准强制例外，但不是谋杀共犯。
- Crown claim or renunciation; keep or release compulsory oath; preserve or end the Day. Releasing the oath allows volunteers. After true ending no immortal king or inherited compulsion. / 坚持或放弃王位主张、保留或解除强制军誓、延续或终止永昼；解除军誓允许志愿者。真结局不能继续永生统治或继承强制权。
- Stages I E00/E01; II adds E02–E04; III adds E05–E08 with actionable knowledge/access; IV after witnessing an end-of-Day outcome grants release knowledge for a later success. / 第一阶段E00/E01；第二阶段新增E02–E04；第三阶段凭可操作知识及通道新增E05–E08；第四阶段亲历永昼终止后获得解约知识，下一次成功可进入真结局。
- Current C.3 campaign proposal: 38 sites + hub, 75 directed edges. The expanded chart is symbolic, not literal scale; its coordinates are provisional, not final art. / C.3现行战役提案：38地点另加据点，75条有向边；扩展图为象征性表达，坐标暂定，非最终美术。
- B.2 adds the royal mother's natural death, the royal age-of-rule law and the five offices' conspiracy, deepens all 13 principal characters; these additions are working fiction. / B.2新增王后自然死亡、王室亲政法及五机构共谋，深化13核心人物；新增皆暂定原创。

- B.3 adds twelve working personal arcs (S01–S12), with persistent knowledge and run-local actions separated. No side-story locks main endings; the Ferryman recognition and K07 remain guaranteed. / B.3新增十二条暂定人物支线，记忆与本轮行动分离；支线不锁主线结局，渡魂人知情确认与K07仍为保底。
