# C.1.1 — The Crownsea: Geography, the Last Voyage and Campaign Passages
# C.1.1 — 王冠海：地理、最后航行与讨伐航路

## 1. The shape of the realm / 王国形态

Alderreach grew around the Crownsea, a sheltered inland sea separated from the Grey Sea by a broken belt of islands. The Frostcrown mountains feed rivers and timber country; the southern Grainlands feed the ports. Sunhold occupies the high eastern shore and the finest deep-water harbour, not the geographical centre. Its unnatural light is local, and winter warmth reaches only what the sacred-flame network supplies; the kingdom beyond still has nights and seasons. The crown consolidated the realm by controlling navigable passages rather than by owning every island. / 奥德里奇围绕王冠海发展；破碎岛链把这片受遮蔽的内海与灰海隔开。霜冠山脉滋养河流与林区，南方粮地供养港口。王城位于东岸高地，占据最优深水港而非地理中心。永昼之光只覆盖王都，冬季供暖只到达圣火网络覆盖之处；其余地方仍有昼夜四季。王室依靠控制可航行水道统一王国，不必拥有每座岛。

The old Winter Palace, ruined royal causeways, submerged memorial statues and successive generations of forts record earlier attempts to unite the sea. Some ruins predate the Unending Day. They are evidence of past economies, wars and engineering, not a new magical conspiracy. / 寒宫遗址、旧王堤、沉水纪念巨像和历代海防工事记录了王国统一内海的过程。一些遗址早于永昼；它们体现经济、战争与工程历史，不增加新的魔法阴谋。

## 2. The last voyage / 最后航行

Edmund's relief convoy left Sunhold bound for the impoverished outer islands. Its normal passage followed charted inner waters and crossed the outer island belt near an established pilot station. Agnes's office delayed part of the food and reserve supplies; local shortages made the mission urgent. Roderick issued a false warning that the regular passage was unsafe and sent the convoy to Bellgrave Roads, a sheltered waiting anchorage on the seaward side of the island belt. It was a plausible temporary diversion, not an inexplicable remote destination. / 埃德蒙的赈粮船队从王都启航，目的地是贫困的外海群岛。正常路线经过内海，在有引航站的水道穿出岛链。阿格尼丝的部门拖延部分粮食及备用补给；当地短缺迫使行动加快。罗德里克以正常航道不安全为由发出虚假通知，将船队引往岛链外侧的沉钟外锚地等待。这是看似合理的临时改航，而非莫名其妙的偏远目的地。

Bellgrave Bay is a side inlet of those roads. Its headlands shelter ships from the prevailing swell, but its narrow navigable outlet lies beneath a naval signal post. Paid mercenaries attacked the convoy while Hugh's forces denied escape and rescue. Edmund stayed with the transports. The flagship sank inside the bay; some other wrecks lie along the nearby coast. The wreck did not drift across the kingdom. / 沉钟湾为该外锚地的支湾，岬角能遮蔽主要涌浪，但狭窄的可航出口受海军信号站控制。佣兵袭击船队，休的部队阻断撤离和搜救；埃德蒙留守运输船。旗舰沉于湾内，其他残骸散落附近岸线，并非横跨王国漂来。

The ban on rescue and the later condemnation explain why the wrecks and many bodies remained. The Ferryman intercepted the condemned dead before the Seal could consume them; the raised wreck became the hub after Eleanor's coronation. The anchor is before the first undead sortie, not before the murder. The plot remains primarily political. / 禁救令及后来的定罪解释了残骸和许多遗骨为何留存。渡魂人在秘印吞噬被定罪死者前截下亡魂；埃莉诺加冕后升起的旗舰成为据点。回溯锚点在亡灵首次出征前，非谋杀前。阴谋依然主要出于政治动机。

## 3. Why an assault cannot sail straight to the palace / 为何不能直航宫城

The returned company begins with small recovered craft, not the intact royal fleet. Its soldiers can survive immersion, but their equipment, formations and command cannot cross open water without transport. Sunhold's deep approaches are watched by signal towers, patrols and shore defences; a small flotilla cannot safely sail past every defended passage. Shallow channels can bypass some large ships but have their own local landing defences. The player seizes passages and useful landing sites, not every settlement along a coast. No arbitrary magical lock requires killing a particular lord. / 回归军团最初只有小型回收船艇，而非完整王家舰队。士兵不惧溺死，但装备、编制和指挥不能脱离运输工具横渡开阔水面。王城深水入口受信号塔、巡逻舰和岸防监控，小船队不能安全绕过全部防线。浅水航道可避开部分大舰，却也有当地登陆守备。玩家夺取航道和有用登陆点，不必攻克沿岸每个居民点；不存在必须击杀特定领主才会开启的任意魔法锁。

Fixed opening: Bellgrave Bay (hub) → Wrecker's Rest (nearby salvage island and abandoned landing). The first victory secures access to local transport and an exit from the bay. Subsequent routes are drawn from actual coastal channels. Knowledge can reveal a passage; controlling it, recruiting pilots and acquiring papers must happen anew in each run. / 固定开场：沉钟湾（据点）→ 断桅岛（附近打捞岛与废弃登陆处）。首胜取得本地运输条件及湾外通路。之后从真实岸线水道中选择；知识可揭示通路，但控制水道、招募引航员、取得文书均须本轮完成。

## 4. Passage families / 航路群

These are overlapping geographical corridors, not classes or parallel levels. Intermediate sites may be skipped where an alternative passage exists. / 以下为相互交叠的地理走廊，不是职业分类或平行关卡层级。有替代通路时可跳过中间地点。

|---|---|---|

| The Grainwater | Lantern Isle → river-mouth sluices → grain islands → Crown Roads or grain basin. Sheltered barge channels, levy records and practical winter supplies. | 粮河水道：灯塔岛→河口水闸→粮岛→王冠海道或粮运港；内河驳运、征发证据和冬储。 |
| The Mourning Passage | Lantern Isle → burial shoals → Pale Abbey → processional landing. Shallow tidal channels used by funerary boats; later branches reveal restricted consignments. | 葬海水道：灯塔岛→古葬浅滩→苍白圣堂→礼仪码头；浅水葬运通路，后期揭示特殊运魂货运。 |
| The Old King's Way | Discovered later: old harbour and submerged causeway remnants link grainwater and northern waters through surviving short ferry crossings. | 旧王途：后期发现，旧港与沉没王堤残段间仍有短渡线，可连接粮河与北部水域。 |

The military and commercial corridors meet at the inner roads; the grain route can join the same roads or use the grain basin; burial channels join the southern estuary at the shoals. No arbitrary connection across a mountain or reef. The last route must always include an available capital landing and palace confrontation. / 军事与商业路线在内海锚地汇合；粮道可汇入该处或进入粮运港；葬运线在浅滩与南部河口相接。不允许随意跨山或礁区连线。每轮始终保留至少一个可达王都登陆点及宫城对质。

## 6. Growth and narrative consistency / 成长与叙事一致性

Earlier routes remain usable as new knowledge reveals tidal passages, disused ports and restricted access. New encounters offer different tactical problems, historical evidence and build opportunities; not merely longer runs. The first victory path must not require the late Seal sites. The same coast exists in every Return. No previously destroyed fort remains destroyed. The prince's knowledge changes his available approach. / 后期知识揭示潮汐水道、废弃港口及受限入口，早期路线仍可使用。新地点提供不同战术问题、历史证据与构筑机会，不只是延长一局。首次成功路线不依赖后期秘印地点。每轮岸线相同；摧毁的堡垒不会跨轮保持毁坏，王子的知识改变可用手段。

Before finalising the atlas, draw a consistent coastline and hydrology, locate all navigable edges, trace the original relief voyage including the diversion, and test each campaign stage for reachability. Then revise B.1's place references and the A.5 site register together. / 定稿前先画一致岸线及水系、定位可航边、标出赈粮船队原航路及改航段，验证各章节可达性。随后同步更新 B.1 人物地点引用和 A.5 地点索引。
