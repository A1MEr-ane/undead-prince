## 1. Campaign grammar / 战役语法

The fleet begins with salvage boats. Every expedition secures Wrecker’s Rest, then sails through connected coastal passages, ferry lines or defended straits. A directed edge represents a forward expedition decision, not a physical one-way current: a real ship could turn around, but the campaign abstracts the cost of retreat into the Return. Detours offer resources and evidence at the cost of another encounter; no artificial equal-length columns. / 船队由打捞小艇起步，每轮先取得断桅滩，然后沿相连的沿岸航道、渡线或设防海峡推进。有向边代表战役前进决策，不是单向洋流；现实船可掉头，但战役将撤退成本抽象为回溯。绕行可多取资源与证据，也多一次遭遇，不人为设置对称层级。

The original relief voyage ran outward toward Northhaven; false navigation orders diverted it to Bellgrave, where a sheltered pocket became a trap. The campaign heads inward toward Sunhold and only partly retraces that voyage. Northhaven is an optional detour: the fleet did not reach it alive. / 生前船队由王都向北望港运粮；伪造改航令使其进入沉钟湾，避风湾变成陷阱。讨伐反向深入王都，仅部分重走旧航路。北望港是可选绕行，生前舰队并未抵达。

## 3. Unlocks and rewards / 解锁及收益

Acts I–IV follow A.5 exactly. Early fragments expose the conspiracy; the capital papers complete K01. Stage II offers family, social and historical sites; Stage III provides K04–K06 sources and an actual current-history route into the Seal, with the guaranteed palace fallback. Stage IV adds no forced geography gate. An optional side story must never block a main ending. / 四幕遵循A.5。前期线索支援谋杀取证，宫廷档案补齐K01。第二幕开放家庭、社会及历史地点；第三幕提供K04–K06来源与本轮秘印通道，保留宫廷保底。第四幕不新增强制地理门槛。支线不锁主结局。

Rewards are candidate classes, not tuned numerical bonuses. A battle normally offers a small draft; a lore site exchanges risk for a modest relic, rest or information; the hub and capital entries never grant a free combat draft. The player sees next encounter type, expected reward family and route rationale, not undiscovered late spoilers. Long routes must incur meaningful risk or supply/time costs; short routes may have stronger garrisons. Actual combat economy is not validated here. / 收益是候选类别，非已平衡数值。战斗通常提供小型构筑选择；探索用较低风险换取轻量遗物、休整或情报；据点和王都入口不白送战斗构筑。玩家预览遭遇类型、收益类别及通行理由，不提前展示未知后期剧透。长路线需要实际风险或补给时间成本，短线守军可更强。战斗经济尚未验证。

## 5. State and implementation / 状态及实现

Only memory and learned skills persist. Site control, preserved stores, physical documents, witness consent, local ally commitments and route assets reset. K01 fragments do not equal the complete conspiracy; K02 at audience, K03 at muster, K04–K06 through Stage III records with capital fallback, K07 after witnessing an end-of-Day history. Use `B/SIDE-STORY-INDEX.json` as the actual scene-condition source; a hook listed on a site card is a candidate scene, not an automatic flag grant. / 仅记忆及习得技艺保留。占领、保全粮储、实物文书、证人同意、盟友承诺及航路资产重置。K01片段不等于完整阴谋；K02由会面、K03由集结、K04–K06由第三幕调查并由宫廷保底，K07在亲历永昼终止后取得。支线以B的JSON作为真实条件来源；地点卡的关联仅是候选场景，不自动发放标记。

Capital route effects are run-local and conditional: navy support can change guards; intact grain reserves and transport influence winter allocation; a valid winter-port plan takes time to implement; current ritual access changes the approach, not the ending availability. Remaining councillors must have individually stated fates. The chart prototype uses click-to-resolve successes, not these full tactical systems. / 王都航路后果是本轮且有条件：海军支持改变宫卫；完整粮储及运输影响冬季分配；冬港计划需要时间落实；本轮仪式入口改变进路，不锁结局。未见议员分别记录去向。网页原型以点击模拟成功，尚未实现完整战术系统。
