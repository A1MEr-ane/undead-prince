**Production specifications, not generated or approved art. / 制作规格，非已生成或批准成品。**

The kingdom is a large maritime country. The campaign chart is a symbolic parchment map, not an isometric physical miniature; separate icon scale from landform scale. Use top-down ink coastlines, abstract mountains, small town symbols and open sea; no shared perspective, large cliff diorama or literal building-to-island ratio. The C.3 HTML and JSON preserve routes and location IDs; label positions may change only with a verified crosswalk. Avoid printing routes or labels on the background; the interface overlays English names, available connections and progress. / 王国是大型海洋国家。战役图为符号化羊皮纸地图，不是等距实体微缩景观；地标图标尺度与地貌尺度独立。俯视墨线海岸、抽象山脉、小型城镇符号及辽阔水域；不采用统一透视、巨型悬崖沙盘或真实建筑岛屿比例。C.3 HTML与JSON保留航路和地点ID；坐标调整须验证映射。底图不印航路及文字，界面覆盖英文地名、可选连线与进度。
