## 2. `docs/canon/A/WORLD-BIBLE.md`

### 2.1 §2 王都的光与热（第 34–36 行）

- **原句**：`... enjoys supernatural uninterrupted daylight and an unusually ice-free harbour; the rest of the kingdom has ordinary seasons, night and winter.` / `……拥有超自然的持续白昼及少结冰的港口；其他地方仍有正常昼夜、季节和寒冬。`
- **新句**：`... lies under the supernatural, uninterrupted light of the Unending Day and keeps an unusually ice-free harbour, warmed by the sacred flame; natural day and night still turn beyond the city, the Unending Day gives light but no heat, and the rest of the kingdom has ordinary seasons and winter, relying on sacred fire to survive the cold.` / `……处于永昼之日超自然的长明光照下，港口靠圣火供暖而少结冰；城外自然昼夜仍在，永昼之日只发光、不供热，其他地方仍有正常季节与寒冬，靠圣火过冬。`
- **依据**：R2 §2（双日、自然昼夜、光热分离）、§3（圣火供暖，包括港口防冻）。

### 2.2 §2 王室纹章（第 38–40 行）

- **原句**：`The royal arms are a gold eight-rayed sun on white.` / `王室纹章为白底金色八芒太阳。`
- **新句**：`The royal arms are a gold eight-rayed sun on white, adopted after the Unending Day; the dynasty's older arms were a guiding star above the waves (design pending).` / `王室纹章为白底金色八芒太阳，是永昼之后的纹章；王朝旧纹章为星与浪（图样待定）。`
- **依据**：R2 §6（宗教与王室早于永昼；王朝原始纹章为海与星，永昼后改为八芒日）、§9.4（用户已批准旧纹章方向）。

### 2.3 §4 秘印起源与圣火下乡（第 87–89 行）

- **原句**：`... then civic prestige. Residual soulfire is inadequate ...` / `……最后还有城市威望。残余冥火不足以……`
- **新句**：`Each winter the churches of the realm received sacred fire from the capital; the public rite was a gift of divine grace, while the flame itself was drawn from the Seal.` / `王都每年冬天还把圣火送往各地教堂：公开的说法是神恩下乡，火种实际由秘印分出。`
- **依据**：R2 §3（圣火龛、冬至请火、公开/隐秘分层）。

### 2.4 §5 解约机制（第 127–129 行）

- **原句**：`the Seal and Return share the principle of interrupting passage, but neither powers the other. By witnessing a late history in which the Seal is stopped, Edmund learns how a binding can be released without consuming the person.` / `秘印与回溯共享中断渡海的原理，却不互相供能。王子亲历至少一次秘印被终止的未来后，掌握如何在不消耗人格的情况下解开束缚。`
- **新句**：`the Seal and the Return are separate bindings; neither powers the other, and ending the Day does not end the Return, destroy Edmund, or open the True Ending by itself. By witnessing the Seal release its captives, Edmund first understands that a binding can be laid down without consuming the person. This is a change of understanding, not a mechanical dependency: the right to release his pact has always been his, though he did not know it.` / `秘印与回溯是两套彼此独立的束缚，互不供能；终止永昼既不会让回溯停止，也不会让王子消散，更不会自动进入真结局。王子在秘印前亲眼看到灵魂被释放，才第一次理解：束缚可以被放下，而不必消耗被束缚者。这是认知上的契机，不是机制上的依赖；解约的权利一直在王子手里，只是他此前不知道。`
- **依据**：R2 §5（回溯是王子和渡魂人的私人契约；永昼不是契约条件；共享原理改为认知契机）。

## 3. `docs/canon/A/NARRATIVE-DESIGN.md`

### 3.1 §10.1 解约认知（第 365–367 行）

- **原句**：`Edmund understands how a binding can be opened without destroying its subject. ... The two bindings share a principle, not a fuel source.` / `埃德蒙理解如何在不消灭主体的情况下解除束缚。……两种束缚共享原理，但不共用燃料。`
- **新句**：`Edmund first understands that a binding can be laid down without destroying its subject. The insight is a precedent in understanding, not a mechanism the two bindings share: the right to release his own pact has always been his, though he did not know it.` / `埃德蒙第一次理解：束缚可以被放下，而不必消灭被束缚者。这是认知上的先例，不是两套束缚共享的机制；解约的权利一直在王子手里，只是他此前不知道。`
- **依据**：R2 §5。

### 3.2 E05 尾声（第 315–317 行）

- **原句**：`The first sunset falls over an undead king and working docks.` / `首次日落照耀亡灵君主和仍在工作的码头。`
- **新句**：`The first sunset after the Unending Day falls over an undead king and working docks. Across the realm, towns burn wood and peat for the first winter in living memory; the outer islands and the capital are at last equally cold and equally dark.` / `永昼结束后的第一次日落照耀亡灵君主和仍在工作的码头。全国城镇在这个冬天第一次烧起木柴和泥炭；外岛与王都终于一样冷、一样黑。`
- **依据**：R2 §8（E05–E08 尾声改写全国：城镇烧木柴和泥炭，外岛与王都一样冷、一样黑）。

### 3.3 E06 尾声（第 327–329 行）

- **原句**：`The king signs a winter budget by candlelight.` / `国王在烛光下签署冬季预算。`
- **新句**：`The king signs a winter budget by candlelight while towns across the realm burn wood and peat; the outer islands and the capital are at last equally cold and equally dark.` / `国王在烛光下签署冬季预算，全国城镇烧起木柴和泥炭；外岛与王都终于一样冷、一样黑。`
- **依据**：R2 §8。

### 3.4 E07 尾声（第 339–341 行）

- **原句**：`The siblings meet on the shore at the end of the first winter preparations.` / `首轮冬季准备结束时兄妹在岸边相见。`
- **新句**：`The siblings meet on the shore at the end of the first winter preparations, while towns across the realm burn wood and peat and the outer islands and the capital are at last equally cold and equally dark.` / `首轮冬季准备结束时兄妹在岸边相见，全国城镇烧起木柴和泥炭，外岛与王都终于一样冷、一样黑。`
- **依据**：R2 §8。

### 3.5 E08 尾声（第 351–353 行）

- **原句**：`Eleanor sees the colour leave the white city walls.` / `妹妹看着白色城墙逐渐失去光彩。`
- **新句**：`The Unending Day goes out. After the natural sun sets, night falls completely over Sunhold for the first time, and the city sees a sky full of stars. ... Across the realm, towns burn wood and peat; the outer islands and the capital are at last equally cold and equally dark.` / `永昼之日熄灭。自然太阳落山后，王都第一次迎来完整的黑夜，第一次看见满天星。……全国城镇烧起木柴和泥炭；外岛与王都终于一样冷、一样黑。`
- **依据**：R2 §2（E08“第一次日落”新含义：不是太阳第一次落下，而是永昼熄灭后王都第一个完整黑夜与星空）、§8（全国尾声）。

### 3.6 §12 实现约定（第 442、445 行）

- **原句**：`Current expedition / 本轮 | run_id, ... aftermath_seen` / 本轮字段无昼夜与军团状态。
- **新句**：本轮字段增加 `time_of_day`、`legion_state`；新增规则段：`Combat occurs only at natural night. By day the Prince may act in diplomacy, meetings, investigation and administration, but cannot use battle powers. At sunrise echoes disperse; the old company and the original fleet dead sink with the fleet, which sails submerged by day and rises after dark. The Unending Day is artificial light and does not count as natural daylight.` / `战斗只在自然夜晚发生。王子白天可以外交、会面、调查和理政，但不能施展战斗力量。日出时回声散去，旧部与最初的舰队亡魂随舰队沉入水下；舰队白天在水下航行，入夜再浮起。永昼之日是人造光，不算自然白天。`
- **依据**：R2 §2（永昼不算自然白天）、§4（王子白天可行动；军团白天沉潜，入夜浮起）。
